1. Cardinal Raycasting

Casts visual sightlines in 4 cardinal directions until hitting a wall. Evaluates ghost velocity vectors to distinguish active approaching threats from ghosts wandering away.

2. Turn-Aware Graph Topology

Performs localized BFS on reachable subgraphs to distinguish open L-bends with escape branches from true sealed cul-de-sacs with approaching ghosts.

3. Geodesic Maze Gradients

Replaces straight-line Manhattan distances with BFS geodesic maze distances, creating continuous 1, 2, and 3-step danger signals around blind corners.
