How HumanVision AI Operates

Combining classic Q-learning with spatial raycasting and graph topology analysis

⚡

Cardinal Raycasting

Casts rays in $N, S, E, W$ until hitting walls. Distinguishes ghosts that are actively approaching vs. retreating, allowing proactive corridor kiting before dangerous encounters occur.

🔄

Turn-Aware Topology

Performs bounded BFS on the reachable subgraph to tell the difference between an $L$-shaped bend leading to open junctions vs. a sealed cul-de-sac with ghost-blocked exits.

🎯

Multi-Step Gradients

Uses true BFS maze geodesic distance to provide continuous danger signals (1-step, 2-step, 3-step) around blind corners, eliminating wall-blindness.
